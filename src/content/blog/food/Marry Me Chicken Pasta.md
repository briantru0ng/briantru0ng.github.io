*Golden seared chicken in a creamy sun-dried tomato Parmesan sauce, tossed with protein pasta.*

**Calories:** 650 | **Protein:** 55g | **Carbs:** 60g | **Fat:** 25g

**Servings:** 4 (1 plate each)

## Ingredients

* 1 lb boneless skinless chicken breast (450g), cut into bite-sized pieces
* 2 tbsp olive oil (30ml)
* Salt & black pepper to taste
* 8 oz protein pasta (e.g. chickpea, lentil, or high-protein blend) (225g)
* 1 tbsp butter (14g)
* 3 cloves garlic, minced
* 1/2 cup sun-dried tomatoes, chopped (80g)
* 1 tbsp tomato paste (15g)
* 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth (240ml)
* 1/2 cup light cream or half-and-half (120ml)
* 3/4 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese (75g)
* 1 tsp Italian seasoning
* 1/2 tsp paprika
* Fresh basil or parsley for garnish

## Steps

1. **Cook the Protein Pasta:** Bring a pot of salted water to a boil and cook the protein pasta according to package instructions until al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup pasta cooking water, then drain.
2. **Season & Sear the Chicken:** Pat the chicken pieces dry, season with salt & pepper. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, heat olive oil and sear the chicken pieces until golden and cooked through, about 4 minutes per side. Remove from skillet and set aside.
3. **Prepare the Sauce:** In the same skillet, add butter. Once melted, add garlic and saute for ~30 seconds until fragrant. Stir in sun-dried tomatoes and tomato paste. Pour in chicken broth and bring to a simmer.
4. **Make It Creamy:** Lower the heat to medium and stir in the light cream (or half-and-half). Add Parmesan cheese, Italian seasoning, and paprika. Stir until the sauce is smooth and slightly thickened.
5. **Combine Everything:** Return the chicken to the skillet along with the cooked pasta. Toss gently so the pasta and chicken are coated in the sauce. Add reserved pasta cooking water a little at a time if the sauce is too thick.
6. **Garnish & Serve:** Sprinkle fresh basil (or parsley) on top and extra Parmesan if desired. Serve immediately.
