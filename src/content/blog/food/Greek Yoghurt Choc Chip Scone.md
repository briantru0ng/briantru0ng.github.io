*A quick and easy single-serve scone made with Greek yoghurt and chocolate chips — air fryer or oven friendly.*

*Recipe from [cakeontherun](https://www.instagram.com/cakeontherun/).*

**Servings:** 1 large scone

## Ingredients

* 3 heaped tbsp (60g) self raising flour (or plain flour + 1/2 tsp baking powder)
* 2 tbsp (50g) Greek or soya yoghurt
* Handful chocolate chips

## Steps

1. Mix everything in a bowl to make a thick dough.
2. Use your hands to squeeze the dough into a ball. It should be soft and smooth, not sticky. Add more flour if needed.
3. Flatten it out into a triangular shape using your hands.
4. Air fry on 180°C for 12–15 mins or oven bake on 180°C for 30 mins. If it's still doughy in the middle, turn it over and cook for a further 3–5 mins.

## Notes

* **Gluten free:** Use gluten free self raising flour + 1/3 tsp xanthan gum.
