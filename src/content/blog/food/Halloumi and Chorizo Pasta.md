*Crispy halloumi and rendered chorizo tossed with pasta in a smoky, creamy tomato sauce.*

**Calories:** 690 | **Protein:** 38g | **Carbs:** 59g | **Fat:** 34g

**Servings:** 1 (1 bowl)

## Ingredients

### For the Pasta

* 3 oz dry short pasta such as cavatappi or macaroni (85g)
* Salt for boiling water

### For the Halloumi & Chorizo

* 3 oz halloumi cheese, cubed (85g)
* 2 oz chorizo sausage, diced (55g)
* 1/2 tsp olive oil if needed (2.5 ml)

### For the Sauce

* 1/2 small yellow onion, finely diced (50g)
* 2 cloves garlic, minced (6g)
* 1/2 cup crushed tomatoes or passata (120g)
* 2 tbsp light cream or half-and-half (30 ml)
* 1/4 tsp smoked paprika (0.5g)
* Black pepper to taste

### For Finishing

* 1 tbsp grated Parmesan cheese (8g)
* Fresh basil or parsley, optional

## Steps

1. **Cook the Pasta:** Bring a pot of salted water to a boil and cook pasta according to package instructions until al dente. Reserve 1/4 cup pasta water, then drain.
2. **Crisp the Chorizo:** Heat a skillet over medium heat, add chorizo, and cook for 3 to 4 minutes until crispy and rendered. Remove chorizo and set aside.
3. **Sear the Halloumi:** In the same pan, add halloumi cubes and cook for 2 to 3 minutes per side until deeply golden. Remove and set aside with the chorizo.
4. **Build the Sauce:** Lower heat to medium, add onion to the pan and cook for 2 minutes until softened. Add garlic and cook for 30 seconds. Stir in crushed tomatoes, cream, smoked paprika, and black pepper. Simmer for 3 to 4 minutes.
5. **Combine Everything:** Add cooked pasta, halloumi, chorizo, and a splash of reserved pasta water to loosen the sauce. Toss until well coated and glossy.
6. **Finish and Serve:** Transfer to a bowl, top with Parmesan and fresh herbs if desired, and enjoy hot.
