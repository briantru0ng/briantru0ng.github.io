*A rich, savory miso broth with ground meat, mushrooms, corn, and ramen noodles — topped with soft-boiled eggs and green onions.*

**Calories:** 610 | **Protein:** 32g | **Carbs:** 70g | **Fat:** 22g

**Servings:** 4 (1 bowl each)

## Ingredients

* 2 tablespoons neutral oil (30ml)
* 8 oz ground chicken or pork (225g)
* 8 oz shiitake mushrooms, stems removed, thinly sliced (225g)
* 1 medium shallot, minced (70g)
* 4 garlic cloves, minced (12g)
* 1-inch piece fresh ginger, grated (15g)
* 3–4 tablespoons white miso paste (45–60g)
* 2 tablespoons low-sodium soy sauce (30ml)
* 1 tablespoon hot chili oil, plus extra for serving (15ml)
* 6–8 cups low-sodium chicken or vegetable broth (1.5–2L)
* Ramen noodles, fresh or dried (4 servings / about 350g)
* 1 cup sweet corn, frozen or canned (150g)
* 2 large soft-boiled eggs, halved (100g)
* 3–4 green onions (scallions), thinly sliced (30g)
* 1 tablespoon sesame seeds (9g)
* 2 cups bok choy or baby spinach leaves, optional (100g)

## Steps

1. **Cook the Meat:** Heat half of the neutral oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Add the ground meat and cook until browned and cooked through, breaking into small pieces. Remove meat and set aside.
2. **Cook the Mushrooms:** In the same pot, add remaining oil. Add mushrooms and cook until softened and golden. Add shallot, garlic, and ginger; saute until fragrant (about 1–2 minutes).
3. **Pour in the Broth:** Stir in miso paste (dissolve it first in a small amount of hot broth if needed), soy sauce, and hot chili oil. Bring the mixture to a simmer.
4. **Add Corn and Meat:** Add corn and return cooked meat to the pot. Allow flavors to meld for several minutes. If using greens like bok choy or spinach, add them now so they wilt.
5. **Cook the Noodles:** Meanwhile, cook ramen noodles in a separate pot according to package. Drain.
6. **Serve:** Divide cooked noodles among bowls. Ladle hot broth mixture over the noodles. Top with halved soft-boiled eggs, sliced green onions, sesame seeds, and extra chili oil if desired. Serve immediately.
