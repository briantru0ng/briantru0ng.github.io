*Air-fried crispy chicken tossed in a sticky honey-garlic sauce, served with jasmine rice and stir-fried noodles.*

**Calories:** 420 | **Protein:** 26g | **Carbs:** 52g | **Fat:** 10g

**Servings:** 4 (1 plate each)

## Ingredients

### For the Chicken & Vegetables

* 1 lb chicken breast (454g), cut into bite-sized chunks
* 3 tbsp potato starch (24g)
* 1/2 tsp salt
* 1/2 tsp black pepper
* 1 tbsp olive oil (15ml) for light spray
* 1 medium carrot, sliced thin (60g)
* 1 green bell pepper, cut in small chunks (120g)

### For the Honey-Garlic Sauce

* 3 tbsp honey (45ml)
* 3 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce (45ml)
* 1 tbsp rice vinegar (15ml)
* 3 cloves garlic, minced
* 1/2 tsp grated ginger
* 1/4 tsp chili flakes
* 1/4 cup chicken broth (60ml)
* 1 tbsp potato starch (8g) + 2 tbsp water (30ml) for slurry

### For the Rice

* 1 cup cooked jasmine rice (185g total)

### For the Stir-Fried Noodles

* 4 oz flat rice noodles (115g), pre-cooked or soaked
* 1 small carrot, julienned (40g)
* 2 stalks green onion, sliced
* 1 tsp sesame oil (5ml)
* 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce (15ml)
* 1/4 tsp garlic powder
* 1/2 tsp black pepper

## Steps

1. **Coat the Chicken:** Pat the chicken dry, season with salt and pepper, then toss with potato starch until evenly coated.
2. **Air-Fry the Chicken:** Preheat air fryer to 390°F (200°C). Lightly spray the basket with olive oil. Air fry chicken 12–14 minutes, shaking halfway, until golden and crispy.
3. **Cook the Vegetables:** In a skillet over medium-high heat, add a small splash of olive oil. Add sliced carrot and green pepper chunks. Stir-fry 3–4 minutes until slightly softened.
4. **Make the Honey-Garlic Sauce:** Add honey, soy sauce, rice vinegar, minced garlic, ginger, chili flakes, and chicken broth directly into the skillet with the vegetables. Bring to a simmer.
5. **Thicken the Sauce:** Add the slurry (potato starch + water) and stir until sauce thickens and becomes glossy.
6. **Combine Chicken and Sauce:** Add the crispy air-fried chicken to the skillet and toss until fully coated with the sticky honey-garlic glaze and veggies.
7. **Cook the Noodles:** In another pan, heat sesame oil. Add julienned carrot and white portion of green onions. Stir-fry 2 minutes. Add cooked noodles, soy sauce, garlic powder, black pepper, and the green onion tops. Toss 1–2 minutes.
8. **Plate the Meal.**
