*Easy, creamy, and completely oven-free. A buttery Biscoff cookie crust topped with a velvety vanilla bean cheesecake filling and finished with a luscious Biscoff drizzle.*

*Recipe from [Archer's Food](https://archersfood.com/no-bake-vanilla-bean-biscoff-cheesecake/).*

**Servings:** 8–10

## Equipment

* Food processor
* Springform pan
* Stand mixer or hand mixer

## Ingredients

### Crust

* 315g Biscoff cookies
* 65g melted butter
* 1 Tablespoon Biscoff spread

### Cheesecake Filling

* 2 vanilla beans
* 400g cream cheese (room temperature)
* 100g powdered sugar
* 50g Biscoff spread
* 200g heavy whipping cream (35% fat or higher)

### Decoration

* 100g Biscoff spread

## Steps

### Crust

1. Blend the Biscoff cookies in a food processor until fine crumbs form.
2. Combine the crumbs with melted butter and Biscoff spread until the texture resembles wet sand.
3. Press firmly into a springform pan, building up the sides first, then freeze for 15–20 minutes.

### Cheesecake Filling

1. Soften room-temperature cream cheese, scrape the vanilla bean seeds into it, and mix until smooth.
2. Fold in the Biscoff spread until combined.
3. In a separate bowl, beat the heavy cream with powdered sugar to soft peaks.
4. Fold the whipped cream into the cream cheese mixture in two batches — be gentle to keep the air in.

### Assembly

1. Spread the filling evenly over the chilled crust.
2. Chill for at least 8 hours (or overnight). Alternatively, freeze for 4–5 hours.
3. Microwave the remaining Biscoff spread until runny and drizzle over the cheesecake. Let set for 5 minutes.
4. Release the springform, slice, and serve cold.

## Notes

* Avoid overwhipping the cream — it can become grainy.
* Heavy cream should be 35% fat or higher for proper whipping.
* Serve cold for the best texture.
