*All the comforting flavors of lasagna in a hearty, creamy soup — topped with a rich ricotta mixture.*

**Calories:** 610 | **Protein:** 42g | **Carbs:** 38g | **Fat:** 30g

**Servings:** 4 (1 bowl each)

## Ingredients

### Ricotta Filling

* 1 1/2 cups whole milk ricotta (340g)
* 1/3 cup grated parmesan (30g)
* Salt and black pepper, to taste
* Dash of nutmeg

### Lasagna Soup

* 1 tbsp olive oil (15ml)
* 1 tbsp unsalted butter (14g)
* 1 tbsp minced garlic (3 cloves)
* 1 lb 90/10 ground beef (454g)
* 1 1/2 tsp Italian seasoning
* 1 1/2 tsp paprika
* 1 tsp red pepper flakes
* 1/2 tsp onion powder
* 1/2 tsp garlic powder
* 1/2 tsp oregano
* 1/4 tsp black pepper
* 1/2 tsp salt, adjust to taste
* 1 jar (24 oz / 680g) marinara sauce
* 1/2 cup heavy cream (120ml)
* 4 cups beef broth (960ml)
* 12 oz lasagna sheets, broken into pieces (340g)
* 2 tbsp parsley, finely chopped
* 1/4–1/3 cup grated parmesan (25–35g)
* 2 tbsp fresh basil, chopped
* 1/2 cup shredded mozzarella (30g), more to taste

## Steps

1. **Make the Ricotta Mix:** In a small bowl, combine ricotta, parmesan, salt, pepper, and a dash of nutmeg. Mix until smooth and set aside.
2. **Cook the Beef:** In a large pot, heat olive oil and butter over medium-high. Add garlic and saute for 1–2 minutes. Add ground beef and all the seasonings. Cook until browned and crumbled.
3. **Build the Soup:** Pour in marinara sauce, heavy cream, and beef broth. Stir and bring to a gentle boil.
4. **Cook the Pasta:** Add broken lasagna sheets to the pot. Cook for 14–16 minutes until pasta is tender.
5. **Finish It:** Stir in basil, parsley, mozzarella, and parmesan. Mix well until melted and creamy.
6. **Serve:** Ladle soup into bowls and top with dollops of the ricotta mixture.
